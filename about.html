About Us

Seekolohiya is the official Psychology Organization of STI Ortigas-Cainta, bringing together students who share a passion for understanding the mind and behavior.

Who we are

We're a community of psychology majors and curious minds alike, built around learning, service, and support. Through talks, workshops, and outreach activities, we connect psychology to everyday student life.

Our mission

To promote mental health awareness within STI Ortigas-Cainta and give students a space to explore psychology beyond the classroom — together.

Our vision

A campus where every student has access to psychological literacy, peer support, and a community that genuinely listens.
