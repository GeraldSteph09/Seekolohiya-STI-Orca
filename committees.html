Committees

What are the committees in Seekolohiya? Here are the committees provided by this organization — five teams that keep everything running, from planning to publishing.

🎨

Creatives Committee

The members of the Creatives Committee are responsible for creating pubmats, posters, and editing for the events and posting. They work together to create the organization's overall theme.

Editors Designers Pubmat Designers
📋

Logistics Committee

Design organizers. Members of the logistics committee are in charge of designing the booths and the whole event. Logistics are also the ones in charge of buying materials to be used during events.

Event/Program Organizer Planner
✏️

Publications Committee

This committee is in charge of writing letters and captions for Seekolohiya. They play the role as writers for Seekolohiya's page.

Caption Writer Script Writer Article Writer
📸

Media Committee

Seekolohiya's media committee is responsible for taking pictures and videos during events and meetings. Each member of the media committee is required to own their own camera.

Photographer Videographer
🎉

Events Committee

The Events Committee are in charge of performing and hosting, as they partake in playing an important role during the event's program flow.

Hosts Performers
